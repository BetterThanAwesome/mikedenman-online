QUICK ANSWERS

THE THINGS AUTHORS
SHOULD ASK FIRST.

Does my book need to be published already?

For this offer, yes. The campaign needs a live destination where a reader can actually purchase the book or audiobook.

Do I need to provide the ad creative?

The base package uses creative you provide. If you need new static or video creative, that can be added separately.

Can you advertise on Reddit or Pinterest?

Yes. They can be added when the audience and content fit the platform. I do not recommend splitting a very small starting budget across too many places at once.

Do you guarantee book sales?

No. Advertising can be built, measured, managed, and improved, but no responsible advertiser can guarantee a specific number of sales from a fixed budget.
